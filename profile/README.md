## 🇬🇧 About OpenVPS

**OpenVPS** is an Egypt-based hosting and infrastructure platform offering fully isolated servers, fast NVMe storage, and a smart control panel for fine-grained management of every service in your environment: servers, domains, email, certificates, and backups.

### Our repositories

| Service | Description | Repository |
|---|---|---|
| 📧 Managed email | Professional mailboxes on your own domain, delivered via CDN | [webmail](https://github.com/OpenVPS/webmail) |
| 🌍 Domains & DNS | Domain registration and DNS management | [domains-dns](https://github.com/OpenVPS/domains-dns) |
| 🛰️ Nameservers | Simple, reliable DNS infrastructure | [Nameserver](https://github.com/OpenVPS/Nameserver) |
| 🔒 SSL & certificates | Encrypt every connection, HTTPS by default | [SSL-Certificates](https://github.com/OpenVPS/SSL-Certificates) |
| 💾 Backup | A backup plan for your infrastructure | [backup](https://github.com/OpenVPS/backup) |
| 🤖 AI Agent | AI-powered automation and management tooling | [Ai-Agent](https://github.com/OpenVPS/Ai-Agent) |
